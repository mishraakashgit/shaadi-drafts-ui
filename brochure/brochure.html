Meet the founder

Shrutika Jaju

Founder & CEO · Architect

Some people know me as Shrutika.

My colleagues know me as an architect.

My family? They’d probably describe me as “slightly dramatic, with suspiciously high standards for details.”

Growing up, I took gift-giving very seriously. Turns out, some things never change. The little girl who obsessed over handmade details found herself obsessing over an entire wedding aesthetic: the invitations, the little notes, the keepsakes, the tiny touches that make a celebration feel personal. Somewhere between the chaos, the excitement and countless design drafts, Shaadi Drafts was born.

Today we create bespoke stationery and thoughtful details for every phase of a celebration, from save-the-dates and invitations to signage, hampers, keepsakes and post-wedding stationery. Because weddings aren’t just about the big moments. They’re about the couple, and the families, big or small.
